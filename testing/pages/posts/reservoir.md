## The gate

The gate on the north side is locked at dusk, but the fence beside it stopped being a fence some years ago. Nobody seems to mind. The heron at the far end certainly doesn't.

There is a sign about swimming, and another sign about the sign. Someone has drawn a small fish on the second one.

## The pumping station

The old pumping station has been about to become apartments for as long as I have lived here. Its windows are the best thing on the walk: tall, arched and full of sky.

The path narrows here, and the reeds lean in after rain. In a wet October you walk it sideways.

### The iron bridge

Past the station, an iron bridge crosses the outflow. It hums when a truck goes over on the road above, and I have stood on it longer than I would like to admit, waiting for one.

## The long way back

There are two ways back. The short one follows the road. The long one goes around the far shore, adds a mile, and has never once been a mistake.

By the time the streetlights come on, the water has gone the same color as the sky, and you can only tell them apart by the ducks.
