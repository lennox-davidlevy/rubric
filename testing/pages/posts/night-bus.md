---
title: The night bus
date: 2026-11-01
tags: [notes, walking]
---

The night bus still runs as far as the gasworks. It is the only bus that has ever waited for me.

## Who rides it

Nurses, mostly, and one man with a cello. We nod. Nobody has ever said a word, and I think everyone would be a little disappointed if they did.

## Where it stops

It stops at the gasworks, turns around in the yard, and sits with its engine running for eleven minutes. From there it is forty minutes on foot, with a flashlight for the last stretch.
