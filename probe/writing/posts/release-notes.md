---
title: What's new in Tally 2.4
---

Tally 2.4 is out today. It's the biggest release since we added sharing last spring, and most of it came straight from your emails.

Heads up: 2.4 needs macOS 13 or newer. If you're on an older Mac, 2.3 keeps working and will get security fixes until June.

## Faster everything

We rewrote how Tally saves your budgets. Opening a big file used to take a few seconds; now it's under half a second. Since the beta, the average sync time has dropped from 4.1 seconds to 0.6, and crashes on launch are down 80%.

## Recurring expenses

You can now mark any expense as recurring, weekly, monthly or yearly, and Tally adds it for you on the day.

## Everything else

- Dark mode follows your system setting
- CSV export includes categories
- Fixed a bug where budgets with emoji in their names wouldn't sync
- The search box remembers your last search

"Recurring expenses alone saved me twenty minutes a month. I didn't know how much I needed it." — Priya, using Tally since 2021

Tally 2.4 is free for everyone with a license. Update from the app, or download it from our site.
