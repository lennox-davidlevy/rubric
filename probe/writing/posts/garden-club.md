title: Garden club news for October
---

Hello everyone, and thank you to the thirty-two people who came to the bulb swap last Saturday. We ran out of tulips in the first hour, which is a good problem to have.

A reminder that dues for next year are due by November 15. It's still $20, and you can pay at any meeting or online.

Our history so far:

1. 1987: Founded by six neighbors in Joan Pike's kitchen.
2. 1995: First plant sale, which raised $140.
3. 2008: Took over the community plots behind the library.
4. 2019: Planted the pollinator garden at the school.
5. 2026: Membership passed 100 for the first time.

This year's numbers are worth sharing. We have 104 members, we gave away about 900 bulbs, and the plant sale raised $3,200 for the school garden.

Upcoming meetings:

- November 4: Putting the beds to sleep, with Dan from the nursery
- December 2: Holiday wreath workshop, bring your own clippers
- January 6: Seed catalog night, snacks provided

Frequently asked questions:

**Do I need to know anything about gardening to join?**
No. Half of us started knowing nothing, and some of us still don't.

**Where are the plots?**
Behind the library on Elm Street, through the gate by the bike rack.

**Can kids come?**
Yes, to everything except the pesticide talk in March.

See you at the next meeting.
