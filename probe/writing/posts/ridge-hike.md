---
title: Three days on the north ridge
---

We left the trailhead at six in the morning, four of us and a dog who had no idea what she was in for.

The first day was all forest. The path climbs steadily through old pines, and you don't see much beyond the next switchback. We stopped at the creek for lunch and Mara fell in, boots and all, which set the tone for the trip.

Day two is the reason anyone does this route. You come out of the trees just after the second camp and the whole valley opens up beneath you. Nobody said anything for a while.

The weather turns fast up there. We watched a storm cross the far side of the valley in about twenty minutes, and if you go, carry a shell even if the morning is clear.

Here is how the three days broke down:

1. Day one: trailhead to Pine Camp, 11 km, 600 m of climbing.
2. Day two: Pine Camp over the ridge to Lake Camp, 14 km, the long one.
3. Day three: Lake Camp down to the road, 9 km, mostly downhill and hard on the knees.

What we would pack differently:

- More water bottles, since the lake is the only refill on day two
- One less stove
- Better socks for Mara
