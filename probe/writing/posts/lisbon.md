---
title: Four days in Lisbon on not much money
---

Lisbon is hilly, cheap by Western European standards, and full of good things to eat for under five euros. Here is what we did with four days and a small budget.

We stayed in Graça, a neighborhood at the top of one of the hills. It's quieter than the center and the rooms are cheaper, but you will climb a lot of stairs.

Tram 28 is the famous one, and it is worth riding once. Go before nine in the morning, though, or you'll spend the ride pressed against a stranger's backpack.

On day two we took the train out to Sintra. It takes forty minutes and costs a few euros each way, and the palaces there look like someone described a castle to a child and then built what the child drew.

The best thing we ate was a bifana, a pork sandwich, from a tiny counter near the fish market. It cost €3.50 and I still think about it.

What we spent, per person, for four days:

| What | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| Room | €140 |
| Food | €95 |
| Transport | €30 |
| Palaces | €25 |

Watch for pickpockets on the trams and around the big squares. Keep your phone in a front pocket.

We are already talking about going back in spring.
