---
title: Week 9 of marathon training
---

This week was the first one where I felt like an actual runner and not someone pretending. My long run was 26 km and I finished it smiling, which has never happened before.

Monday was a rest day. Tuesday I did intervals at the track: eight times 800 m with two minutes walking between. My legs were heavy for the last two but I held the pace, around 4:10 per kilometer.

Note to self: stop eating a whole bagel forty minutes before a run.

The long run on Sunday was along the canal, out and back. The first 15 km felt easy. Around 20 km I started to fade, and then a man on a bike rode alongside for a minute and told me his daughter ran this route every weekend before her first marathon. He rode off before I could ask how she did.

What I'm thankful for this week:

- My knee not hurting
- The guy on the bike
- Cold weather

Total for the week was 58 km, which is the most I've ever run in seven days. Six weeks to go.
