title: What happened on Tuesday
---

On Tuesday afternoon our app was down for about two hours. If you lost work or couldn't get in, I'm sorry. Here's what happened, in plain words.

At 2:10 pm a routine update went out to our servers. It had a mistake in it that made the database refuse new connections, so nobody could log in, and anyone already logged in couldn't save.

We noticed within four minutes, but it took us much longer to understand it. The error messages pointed at the network, and we spent the first hour looking in the wrong place.

1. 2:10 pm: the update goes out.
2. 2:14 pm: alerts fire and the team starts looking.
3. 3:20 pm: we find the real cause.
4. 4:05 pm: the fix is out and everything works again.

Nothing you saved before 2:10 was lost. Anything you typed between 2:10 and 4:05 and tried to save did not reach us, and we can't get it back.

> sorry, I know you said not to text during the outage but my whole thesis draft was in there

That was a message one of you sent our support line, and it's the one that stayed with me. Your thesis was fine, by the way. It had saved at 1:58.

From now on every update goes to a small group of servers first and waits half an hour before going anywhere else. We should have been doing that already.

If you think you lost something, write to us and we will look.
