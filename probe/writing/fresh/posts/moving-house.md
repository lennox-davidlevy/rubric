---
title: We moved, and nothing went to plan
---

We moved last weekend. I had a spreadsheet. The spreadsheet did not survive contact with reality.

The truck was supposed to arrive at 8. It arrived at 11:40, and the driver explained, very kindly, that our street did not exist on his map. Our street has existed since 1952.

By the time everything was loaded it was dark, so we unloaded at the new place by the light of our phones. Somewhere in that process the box with the bed frame bolts disappeared. We slept on the mattress on the floor for three nights.

Things I would tell anyone moving:

- Keep the bolts, the chargers and a kettle in a bag that never goes on the truck
- Label boxes by room on two sides, not the top
- Take a photo of the back of the TV before you unplug anything

The neighbors left a plate of cookies on our step with a note that said welcome, and that the trash goes out on Wednesdays. I have never been so grateful for information about trash.

The bolts turned up on Thursday, in a box labeled "bathroom."

We like it here.
