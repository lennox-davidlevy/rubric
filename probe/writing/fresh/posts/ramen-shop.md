---
title: Noodle Bar on 5th is worth the wait
---

There is always a line outside Noodle Bar, and now I know why.

We went on a Thursday at seven and waited forty minutes on the sidewalk. They don't take reservations, so go early or bring a book. The room is tiny, maybe twenty seats, all at a counter facing the kitchen, which means you get to watch everything.

I had the tonkotsu. The broth is thick and cloudy and tastes like it took two days, which, according to the menu, it did. The noodles were firm, the egg was perfect, and the pork belly fell apart when I touched it with my chopsticks.

My partner had the vegetable miso, which was good but not in the same league. If you don't eat pork, I'd still go, but I'd lower your expectations a little.

My top three things on the menu:

1. The tonkotsu, obviously
2. The pickled cucumbers, which come free while you wait for a seat
3. The black sesame ice cream

It's cash only, which nobody told us, and there's no machine nearby. We had to run four blocks to find one while our food went cold.

Two bowls, a beer and dessert came to $54 before tip.

We are going back next week.
