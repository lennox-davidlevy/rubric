---
title: The library needs volunteers this winter
---

The Eastside branch library is looking for volunteers to help with the winter reading program, which starts on December 1 and runs through February.

Last winter, forty volunteers read with 212 children and gave out more than 1,500 free books. Teachers told us that the kids who came every week read noticeably better by spring.

You don't need any experience. You do need to be over 16 and able to come at the same time every week for at least six weeks, because the children get attached to their reader.

Every volunteer has to pass a background check before their first session, and it takes about two weeks, so please sign up by November 15 if you want to start on the first day.

"I signed up because my daughter loved the program when she was little. I've stayed six years because of the kids." — Marcus, volunteer since 2020

Sessions are on Tuesday and Thursday afternoons from 3:30 to 5:00, and on Saturday mornings from 10 to noon.

To sign up, stop by the front desk or email the branch. We'll send you the forms.
