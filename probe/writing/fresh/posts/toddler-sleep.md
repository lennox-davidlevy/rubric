---
title: How we finally got our son to sleep
---

For eleven months our son woke up every two hours. I am writing this in case it helps one other tired parent, and also so I remember that it ended.

I want to say first that I'm not a doctor and nothing here is advice. Every child is different, and if you're worried, talk to your pediatrician, which we did twice.

What changed things was moving bedtime earlier, not later. Everyone told us a tired baby sleeps better. For our son it was the opposite: when he got overtired, he fought sleep for an hour and then woke more in the night.

We also started doing the same few things every night, in the same order:

1. Bath, short and warm.
2. Pajamas and one bottle in the dim living room.
3. Two books, always ending with the same one.
4. Lights off, a song, and out of the room.

The first three nights were awful. On the fourth night he slept for six hours straight, and I woke up at 3 am in a panic because the house was so quiet.

Some words that got thrown at us a lot, in case they're new to you too:

- **Sleep regression**: a stretch when a baby who slept well suddenly doesn't, often around big developmental jumps.
- **Wake window**: how long a baby can comfortably stay awake between sleeps.
- **Dream feed**: feeding a baby while they're still mostly asleep, before you go to bed.

He is fourteen months now and sleeps eleven hours most nights. Most. Last night he was up at five singing to his stuffed bear.
